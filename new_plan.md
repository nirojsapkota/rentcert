TODAY'S IDEA: BARRIERTRACK
A client-register + automated recall CRM for Victorian pool/spa barrier safety inspectors.

THE PROBLEM & WHO IT'S FOR
Since Dec 2020, every VIC pool/spa owner must register their pool with their local council and get a Certificate of Pool/Spa Barrier Compliance from a registered inspector every 4 years. There are hundreds of small 1-5 person inspection businesses doing this across VIC (I found several real ones in search: Pool Inspections Melbourne, NW Pool Inspections, BPPC, Local Pool Inspections, Pool Safe Compliances). Two concrete pains for these businesses:
1. A client inspected today is due again in ~1,460 days. On a spreadsheet or paper diary, that follow-up quietly falls through the cracks, and the inspector loses repeat revenue to whoever the panicking owner calls when the council finally chases them.
2. VIC has 79 councils, each with its own quirks for receiving/registering the certificate. A sole inspector working across a few municipalities (e.g. Casey/Cardinia/Dandenong, right in your area) has to remember which council wants what.

WHY IT'S A GOOD FIT FOR YOU
It's a standard vertical CRM: client/property register, a scheduled-reminder engine (cron + email/SMS via ClickSend/Twilio, which you've already scoped for a past idea), templated PDF certificate generation, and a documents vault — all comfortably TS/React/Node or Rails + Postgres. No novel technical risk, and it reuses the deadline-tracker-plus-vault pattern you've already designed for RentCert/CertClock, just pointed at a service business instead of the regulated party. Infra cost at low scale: roughly $20-50/month.

MVP SCOPE (2-3 WEEKS CODING, PLUS A SEPARATE 1-2 WEEKS OF DATA WORK)
- Client & property register with inspection history
- Automated recall engine: X months before the 4-year mark, auto-draft (or auto-send) a re-engagement email/SMS to that old client
- A council directory (lodgement method/contact/portal per council) — ship v1 with the ~15-20 biggest metro councils to cover most of the customer base, expand later
- Simple templated PDF compliance certificate with photo upload
- Calendar view of upcoming jobs + recalls due
Skip for v1: route optimization, payroll, accounting integrations, dispatch for multi-inspector teams.
Honest note: researching and data-entering the council directory is actually more time-consuming than the code itself.

MONETIZATION & REALISTIC REVENUE
SaaS subscription: Solo plan ~$39/month, small-team ~$89/month. Plus a one-off $99 "we'll migrate your spreadsheet for you" onboarding fee, which also doubles as a sales qualifier. Realistically: 10 paying customers at ~$45/month average is about $450/month MRR — a solid side-income proof point, not a living wage. This is a slow-grind niche SaaS, not a home run.
